Room Resonance Lab

An interactive prototype for exploring how room geometry shapes natural frequencies and the spatial patterns of acoustic modes.

Exciting a furnished room

A planned standalone prototype comparing how different source signals excite the modes of the same furnished room. The aim is to connect modal analysis to a forced response through examples such as a clap, a whistle, and a balloon pop.
