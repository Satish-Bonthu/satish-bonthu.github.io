# Portfolio editorial guide

## Brand

Use BrBG as the default across the portfolio and professional media. Use deep teal `#003c30`, warm brown `#8c510a`, ivory `#f6f4ee`, and pale mint `#c7eae5`. Reserve a diverging BrBG scale for technical plots with a meaningful midpoint.

## Project entries

Use the following order. Aim for one short sentence per field.

1. **Problem** — What is the engineering question?
2. **Proposed solution** — What approach addresses it?
3. **How it was approached** — What was simulated, tested, or built? Use future tense for planned work.
4. **Level of difficulty** — Give a level and name the technical challenge.
5. **Value and takeaway** — State the decision supported, insight gained, or potential use.
6. **Sustainability** — Explain the relevant impact. Distinguish measured results from potential benefits.

Omit fields when the evidence is unavailable or the field does not apply. Preserve the status of each item and link to its primary source where available.

For unpublished, unreviewed demonstrations, use: “Demonstration only · Unreviewed work. Shared for exploration. Constructive feedback is welcome.” Identify archived manuscripts as preprints and concepts as planned. Use the same status in captions and social posts that accompany the work.
