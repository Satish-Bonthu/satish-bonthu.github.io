Acoustics

Didgeridoo resonance

Problem
How does bore geometry shape an instrument’s resonances?
Proposed solution
Compare bore shapes, including a snake-shaped design.
How it was approached
Explore the acoustic modes of the proposed bore geometries.
Level of difficulty
Intermediate · Bends and length affect the domain geometry.
Value and takeaway
Makes it easier to explore how geometry influences resonance.

Demonstration only · Unreviewed experiment. Constructive feedback is welcome.

Ray optics

Tokarsky’s unilluminable room

Problem
Can a point source illuminate every location in a mirrored room?
Proposed solution
Use ray tracing to explore this geometric puzzle.
How it was approached
Simulate reflected light paths in Tokarsky’s room.
Level of difficulty
Intermediate · Repeated reflections depend on the exact geometry.
Value and takeaway
Makes the geometry behind the paradox easier to explore.

Demonstration only · Unreviewed visualization. Constructive feedback is welcome.
