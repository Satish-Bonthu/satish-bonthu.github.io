Reykjavík, Iceland

Satish Bonthu

I’m a mechanical engineer with over 15 years of CFD consulting experience. My PhD research develops reduced-order models for wave-based room acoustics, with the goal of predicting impulse responses at lower computational cost.

Current work

Reduced-order modeling for room acoustics

I study how reduced models predict time-domain impulse responses as room boundary impedance changes, with particular attention to accuracy and stability.

Room Resonance Lab

An interactive modal analysis prototype for exploring how room geometry shapes acoustic modes and how excitation affects which modes appear in a response.

Selected engineering work

Marine propeller retrofit

Used CFD on an Icelandic project to assess the fuel-saving and greenhouse gas reduction potential of retrofitting a ship’s propeller.

Natural ventilation retrofits

Compared CFD predictions of wind-driven ventilator suction with experimental data and used a passive tracer to study dispersion and removal in a naturally ventilated toilet.

The Cloud Beneath the Ice

Presented research at IGEC 2025 in Reykjavík on the environmental and socioeconomic implications of data center development in Iceland.
