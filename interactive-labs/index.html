Room Resonance Lab

Explore how room geometry changes natural frequencies and the spatial shape of acoustic modes.

About this lab
Problem
Room modes are difficult to picture from frequencies alone.
Proposed solution
Make the pressure patterns and frequencies visible together.
How it works
Adjust the room and explore modal fields, phase animation, and receiver behavior.
Level of difficulty
Intermediate · Interpreting spatial modes and nodal patterns.
Value and takeaway

MATLAB app

Modal Solver

A MATLAB app for computing and inspecting acoustic modes of rooms, including floor plans whose mode shapes are harder to predict from dimensions alone.

Request a guided demo

About this lab
Problem
Irregular room geometry makes natural frequencies and mode shapes difficult to estimate with rectangular-room formulas.
Proposed solution
Use a MATLAB app to bring numerical modal analysis and visualization into one workflow.
How it works
The app runs MATLAB-based solvers and displays the resulting acoustic frequencies and spatial mode shapes.
Level of difficulty
Advanced · Geometry discretization and numerical eigenmode extraction.
Value and takeaway
See how a room’s shape affects where its natural modes concentrate acoustic pressure.
