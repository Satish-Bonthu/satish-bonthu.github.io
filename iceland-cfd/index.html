CFD · Building ventilation

Can the wind ventilate a restroom?

Problem
Naturally ventilated restrooms need a way to clear stale air.
Proposed solution
Use a wind-driven roof ventilator as a retrofit.
How it was approached
Compared simulated suction with measurements and traced contaminant removal in CFD.
Level of difficulty
Advanced · Local airflow determines how quickly a tracer clears.
Value and takeaway
Connects ventilator performance with air removal inside the room.
Sustainability
Explores ventilation driven by wind with lower reliance on powered extraction.
